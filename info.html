Brooke is a graphic designer and strategist specializing in branding, web design and art direction. She transforms ideas into thoughtful visual experiences through strategic thinking, compelling narratives and meticulous craft.

Working across print and digital, her practice explores how typography, layout and visual systems shape the way people connect with brands.

Based in Tampa, Florida, she thrives on solving complex design challenges and crafting engaging user experiences — balancing visual craft with usability to build work that is as intuitive as it is scalable.
